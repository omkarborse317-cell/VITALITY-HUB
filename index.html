OUR MISSION

Building a Healthier Community

Good health is not only about avoiding illness. It is about developing healthy physical, mental and social habits every day.

Stay Active

Encourage regular walking, stretching and simple exercises suitable for different age groups.

Mind Wellness

Promote yoga, breathing exercises, meditation and healthy ways to manage everyday stress.

Eat Healthy

Create awareness about balanced meals, nutritious foods and healthy eating habits.

Community First

Work together with community members to understand their health habits and encourage positive change.

Think about balance, not perfection.

A healthy meal can include a variety of food groups. The exact balance depends on individual needs, preferences and circumstances.

  • Include vegetables and fruits
  • Choose nutritious protein sources
  • Prefer whole grains when possible
  • Drink water regularly
